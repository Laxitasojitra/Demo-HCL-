# Password Reset (Self-Service)

Applies to: corporate (Active Directory / Entra ID) accounts.

## Resetting a forgotten password
1. Go to `https://passwordreset.acme-corp.com` from any device.
2. Enter your corporate email address.
3. Verify your identity with your registered phone number or authenticator app.
4. Choose a new password and confirm it.
5. Wait up to 5 minutes, then sign in again. On a laptop, connect to the office network or VPN so the new password syncs.

## Password rules
- At least 14 characters.
- Cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You receive reminder emails 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

## Changing a password that is about to expire
On Windows press Ctrl+Alt+Del and choose "Change a password". On macOS use the self-service portal above. Stay connected to the VPN while changing it if you are remote.

## After resetting
Update the saved password on your phone (email app, Wi-Fi profile) to avoid repeated failed sign-ins, which can lock your account.

## If self-service does not work
If you never registered recovery methods, the Service Desk must verify your identity by video call before resetting the password.
