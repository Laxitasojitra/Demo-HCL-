# Office Wi-Fi

Applies to: office networks "ACME-Corp" (employees) and "ACME-Guest" (visitors).

## Connecting to employee Wi-Fi
Select "ACME-Corp" and sign in with your corporate email and password. Company laptops connect automatically using a device certificate. Personal phones should use "ACME-Guest".

## Guest Wi-Fi for visitors
Visitors connect to "ACME-Guest" and accept the terms page. Guest access lasts 24 hours. The daily guest password is shown at reception.

## Wi-Fi connected but no internet
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
2. Forget the network and reconnect.
3. On Windows, run `ipconfig /release` then `ipconfig /renew`.
4. Restart the laptop.

## Slow or dropping Wi-Fi
- Move closer to an access point; meeting rooms with closed glass doors have weaker signal.
- Disconnect from the VPN while in the office; it is not needed on "ACME-Corp".
- Use a docking station with a wired network cable at your desk for video calls.

## Report an outage
If several colleagues in the same area lose Wi-Fi at the same time, report it as an outage with your floor and building number.
