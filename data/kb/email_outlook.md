# Email and Outlook

Applies to: Microsoft 365 mailboxes and the Outlook desktop and mobile apps.

## Outlook not receiving or sending email
- Check the status bar at the bottom of Outlook. If it says "Working offline", click Send/Receive > Work Offline to turn it off.
- Check your internet connection and open `https://outlook.office.com` in a browser. If webmail works, the problem is the desktop app.
- Restart Outlook. If it still fails, start it in safe mode with `outlook.exe /safe`.

## Mailbox full
Mailboxes are 50 GB. Empty "Deleted Items", delete large attachments, and turn on Online Archive from File > Mailbox Settings.

## Setting up email on a phone
Install the Outlook mobile app, sign in with your corporate email, and approve MFA. Install Company Portal if prompted; it is required to protect company data.

## Shared mailboxes
To access a shared mailbox (for example support@ or hr@), the mailbox owner must request access for you on the IT portal. It appears in Outlook automatically within 1 hour of approval.

## Out-of-office reply
In Outlook go to File > Automatic Replies, or in webmail go to Settings > Mail > Automatic replies.

## Suspicious email
Do not click links. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook.
