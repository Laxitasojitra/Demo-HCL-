# Requesting Hardware and Accessories

Applies to: laptops, monitors, docking stations, headsets, keyboards, and mice.

## Standard accessories
Headsets, keyboards, mice, and laptop bags can be collected free from the IT Tech Bar on floor 2, Monday to Friday 9:00-17:00. Remote employees can order them on the IT portal under "Accessories"; delivery takes 3-5 business days.

## Monitors and docking stations
Order from the IT portal under "Hardware Request". Every employee is entitled to one docking station and up to two monitors. Additional or non-standard items need manager approval.

## Laptop refresh
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. You receive an email when your laptop is due. To request an early replacement because of damage or poor performance, raise a "Hardware Request" and describe the issue.

## New joiners
Managers request equipment for new joiners at least 10 business days before the start date through the "New Joiner" form.

## Returning equipment
Leavers return all equipment to the Tech Bar or use the prepaid return box sent by IT.
