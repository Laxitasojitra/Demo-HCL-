# Microsoft Teams and Meetings

Applies to: Microsoft Teams desktop, web, and mobile apps.

## Microphone or camera not working in Teams
1. In Teams, go to Settings > Devices and select the correct microphone, speaker, and camera.
2. Make sure the camera privacy shutter is open and the headset is not muted on the cable.
3. On Windows, check Settings > Privacy > Camera/Microphone and allow access for Teams.
4. Close other apps that may be using the camera (Zoom, browser tabs).
5. Restart Teams completely by right-clicking its icon in the system tray and choosing Quit.

## Teams is slow or will not load
Clear the Teams cache: quit Teams, delete the contents of `%appdata%\Microsoft\Teams`, then reopen it. You can also use `https://teams.microsoft.com` in a browser as a temporary workaround.

## Screen sharing shows a black screen
Share a single window instead of the whole screen, and update your graphics driver from Company Portal.

## Booking meeting rooms
Use "Room Finder" in Outlook when creating a meeting. Rooms with Teams displays join the call automatically when you tap "Join" on the room console.
