# Installing Software

Applies to: company-managed Windows and macOS laptops.

## Approved software (self-service)
Employees do not have local admin rights. Install approved apps yourself:
1. Windows: open "Company Portal" from the Start menu.
2. macOS: open "Self Service" from Applications.
3. Search for the app (for example Zoom, Slack, Python, VS Code, Adobe Reader, 7-Zip) and click Install.
Installation usually finishes within 10 minutes. Restart if the app does not appear.

## Software not in Company Portal or Self Service
If the app you need is not listed in the catalog, submit a "Software Request" form on the IT portal with the app name, version, and business reason. Requests are reviewed by Security within 3 business days. Paid software also needs your manager's approval for the license cost.

## Install fails or is stuck
- Make sure you have at least 5 GB of free disk space.
- Restart the laptop and retry the install from the portal.
- Check that you are connected to the internet; installs download from the cloud.

## Updating software
Managed apps update automatically every Tuesday night. To update now, open the portal and click "Reinstall" on the app.
