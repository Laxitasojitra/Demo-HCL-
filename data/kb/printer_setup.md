# Printing

Applies to: office multifunction printers (print, scan, copy). Print system: follow-me printing.

## Adding the office printer
1. Windows: open Company Portal and install "ACME FollowMe Printer".
2. macOS: open Self Service and install "ACME FollowMe Printer".
There is only one printer queue; your job prints on whichever printer you badge into.

## Releasing a print job
Send the job to "ACME FollowMe", walk to any office printer, and tap your employee badge on the reader. Jobs are deleted after 24 hours if not released.

## Print job not showing at the printer
- Make sure you are on "ACME-Corp" Wi-Fi or the VPN.
- Check the job was sent to "ACME FollowMe" and not a home printer.
- Remove and reinstall the printer from the portal.

## Paper jams and toner
Follow the diagrams on the printer screen to clear jams. For low toner or hardware faults, use the "Report issue" button on the printer screen; Facilities replaces toner within 1 business day.

## Scanning to email
At the printer choose Scan > Scan to Me. The PDF is emailed to the badge owner.
