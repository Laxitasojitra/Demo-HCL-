# Slow or Misbehaving Laptop

Applies to: company-managed Windows and macOS laptops.

## Laptop is slow
1. Restart the laptop. Many issues come from not restarting for weeks.
2. Close unused browser tabs and apps; check Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) for apps using high CPU or memory.
3. Make sure at least 15% of the disk is free. Empty the Recycle Bin and clear the Downloads folder.
4. Install pending updates from Settings > Windows Update and restart.

## Laptop overheating or fan is loud
Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface and keep vents clear. Constant high fan noise with no apps open should be reported to IT.

## Battery drains quickly
Lower screen brightness, use the "Balanced" power mode, and check Settings > System > Battery for apps with high usage. Batteries under 70% health are replaced; ask IT to run a battery health report.

## Blue screen or will not start
- Hold the power button for 15 seconds, then turn it on again.
- If BitLocker asks for a recovery key, get it from `https://myaccount.acme-corp.com/devices` on another device.
- Repeated blue screens need a hardware check; contact the Service Desk.

## Physical damage
Cracked screens, liquid spills, and broken keyboards are repaired by IT. Do not try to open the laptop yourself.
